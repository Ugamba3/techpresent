Step-by-Step Breakdown

A closer look at each phase of your ordering journey

01

Browse & Select Your Kit

Head to the Starter Packs or Catalogue and find gear curated for your specific track. Whether you're into Robotics, Web Development, Data Science, or Design — we've got you covered.

  • Filter by your bootcamp track
  • View detailed descriptions and specs
  • Compare prices and bundles
02

Enter Your Details at Checkout

When you're ready, proceed to checkout. We'll need your student name, enrolled course track, and contact information to ensure your kit is prepared correctly.

  • Full name and student ID
  • Course track and batch
  • Phone number for delivery updates
03

Choose Delivery or Campus Pickup

Decide how you want to receive your kit. Pick it up at the CITE TechStars Centre in Choba, or have it delivered straight to your doorstep anywhere in Port Harcourt.

  • Free campus pickup within 24 hours
  • Home delivery within 48 hours
  • Real-time tracking via Track Delivery

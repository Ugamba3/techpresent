Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before enrolling.

Do I need prior coding experience?

Not for our Web Development track — it's designed for absolute beginners. AI/ML and Cyber Security require basic programming familiarity (Python fundamentals). We offer a free prep course for all tracks.

What is the class schedule?

Classes run Tuesday, Thursday, and Saturday. Weekday sessions are 6:30 PM – 9:00 PM WAT, and Saturday sessions are 10:00 AM – 2:00 PM WAT. All sessions are recorded for review.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes. Every graduate receives a verified digital certificate that can be shared on LinkedIn and downloaded as a PDF. Certificates are issued upon project completion and final assessment.

Is there a payment plan?

Absolutely. You can pay in 3 monthly installments for any bootcamp. We also offer a 15% early-bird discount if you register at least 3 weeks before the cohort starts.

What happens if I miss a class?

All live sessions are recorded and uploaded to your student dashboard within 2 hours. You also get lifetime access to the course materials and can join future cohorts for free.
